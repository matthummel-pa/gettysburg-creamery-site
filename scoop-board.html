Owner tool · concept demo

The Scoop Board.

Pack the freezer like you do in the shop: tap a tub, tap a flavor. Publish tonight’s case instantly, or schedule a drop for a future morning. When that date arrives, the homepage flips by itself — no developer, no “can you update the flavors?” text.

1. Pack the case

Click a slot (outlined = selected), then click a flavor chip to drop it in. 32 regular, 4 dairy-free, 4 no-sugar-added.

2. Publish or schedule

Publish writes today’s homepage case in this browser. Schedule queues a lineup; the public site auto-promotes it when that date arrives (checked on every page load).

    3. Flavor photos

    Upload a photo for any flavor. It shows on the homepage tubs, the flavor library, and this board. Images stay in this browser for the demo — a live site would store them with the flavor record in Netlify Blobs or a CMS.

    Production version would save to a CMS or Netlify Blobs so every visitor sees the same case. This concept stores the board in the browser so you can demo it without a backend.
